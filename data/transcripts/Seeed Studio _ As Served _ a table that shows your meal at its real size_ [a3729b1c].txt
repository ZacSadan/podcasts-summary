[SHOW NOTES]
As Served projects each dish onto the table at its real size, so guests see the portion before they order. Pointing and holding for three seconds places the order.
A Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3 on the XIAO Bus Servo Adapter drives the SO-101 arm that delivers the matching utensil, and a reTerminal E1002 shows the menu and order card on e-paper.
