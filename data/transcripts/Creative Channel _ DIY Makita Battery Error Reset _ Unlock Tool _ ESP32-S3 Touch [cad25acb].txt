🔌 WIRING
DATA → GPIO2
ENABLE → GPIO3
B- → GND

Use a 4.7kΩ resistor to pull DATA up to 3.3V.

⚠️ Never pull the DATA line up to 5V and never connect ESP32 GPIO directly to the battery B+ / B- terminals.
