[SHOW NOTES]
In this best-of compilation of "How Crime Works," Business Insider revisits interviews with 12 former gang members about the unwritten rules they had to follow to survive.

Skipp Townsend, a former member of Los Angeles' Rollin 20s Bloods, Alex Sanchez, a former high-ranking member of MS-13 in Los Angeles and El Salvador, and Tyrone White, a former street soldier in LA's 65 Menlo Gangster Crips, share what was expected of them on the streets.

Shanduke McPhatter, a former senior figure in New York's Gangsta Killer Bloods, and Vidal Guzman, a former East Coast Bloods member who was incarcerated at Rikers Island, reflect on gang life in New York. Mike Moy discusses his time in a Chinatown gang before later becoming an NYPD officer.

Andy "Rebirth" Pellerano, a former soldier in the Almighty Latin King Nation in New Orleans, and former London gang member Omar Sharif, share the codes that governed their lives.

Former prison gang members Manuel Medrano of the Arizona-based New Mexican Mafia; John "Boxer" Mendoza, a former high-ranking Nuestra Familia member; and Leroy Smith, formerly of the UK's African Crew prison gang, discuss the rules and power structures behind bars.

Former Mongols Motorcycle Club chapter president Justin DeLoretto describes the expectations of the biker club.
