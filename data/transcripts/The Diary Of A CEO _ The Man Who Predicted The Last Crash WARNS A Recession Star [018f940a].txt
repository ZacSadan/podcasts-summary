[SHOW NOTES]
Are we on the verge of a catastrophic economic recession driven by the AI boom? Legendary investor Steve Eisman sits down to discuss market crashes, bubbles and the next financial crisis, revealing the driving force behind the AI hype and how everyday people can protect their wealth.

Steve Eisman is a renowned professional investor famously known for predicting the 2008 subprime mortgage collapse, a story immortalized in _The Big Short._ Today, he hosts a financial podcast and educational venture alongside his wife, Valerie. Through this platform, he aims to teach everyday people how to understand the markets without falling for Wall Street’s hype.

*In this episode, he explains:*
■ *The Rule of Ruin:* Human incentives will always trump ethics, which is the hidden catalyst behind every major financial collapse.
■ *The AI Extinction Conspiracy:* Tech billionaires are faking hysteria about AI ending the world simply to trigger government regulations that lock out their cheaper international competitors.
■ *The Crypto Illusion:* Cryptocurrency completely fails as an economic hedge because it moves directly in sync with tech stocks rather than protecting against inflation.
■ *The Homeownership Trap:* Buying a house is currently a terrible financial decision, and you are much better off renting while investing your extra cash into the stock market.
■ *The Boring Secret to Wealth:* Everyday people should completely avoid stock picking and instead consistently invest a portion of their income into the S&P 500 index fund for decades.
