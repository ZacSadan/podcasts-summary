[SHOW NOTES]
Brooklyn's Industry City was built more than a century ago to move the world's cargo. Now its upper floors are filling up with robots, medical tech and space hardware.

The 6 million-square-foot campus on the Sunset Park waterfront started as Bush Terminal in the 1890s and sat mostly dormant for decades. Its owners, the developers behind Chelsea Market, spent $450 million modernizing it. About 1,500 people worked there when the redevelopment began in 2013. Today it's about 9,000.
