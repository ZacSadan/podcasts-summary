They made SCUBA SUITS for cockroaches, and you need to see why

Scientists can now remotely control cockroaches by putting electrodes in their body. The goal is to add cameras and have them help search for people in hard to reach places

But some disasters involve flooding…and cockroaches can’t breathe underwater…so they made them 3D printed, watertight, SCUBA SUITS

So NOW….they can move around IN WATER FOR HOURS AT A TIME
