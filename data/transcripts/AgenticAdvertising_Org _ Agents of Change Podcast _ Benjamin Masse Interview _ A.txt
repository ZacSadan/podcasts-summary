[SHOW NOTES]
In the Agents of Change x AgenticAdvertising.org interview series, sponsored by Agnitio, Aubriana Alvarez Lopez sits down at Cannes Lions 2026 with Benjamin Masse, Chief Product Officer at Triton Digital (an iHeartMedia company) and inaugural President of AgenticAdvertising.org. They held a a rapid-fire conversation about where agentic AI is already creating value and where humans must remain firmly in the loop.

Ben shares why his teams already rely on AI agents for software development, why advertising needs to evolve beyond today's "day trading" mindset, and how agentic workflows could transform media buying into long-term portfolio management. He also explains why identity alone is no longer enough to value media, how richer context and metadata change the economics of advertising, and why media mix modeling may become even more important—not less—in an agent-driven future.

The conversation closes with one of the most memorable lines from Cannes:

"Ad tech isn't Software as a Service. It's Forms as a Service."

Ben argues that AI should eliminate repetitive operational work so humans can focus on what they're uniquely good at: judgment, creativity, curiosity, and strategy.

In this episode:

How Triton Digital is using AI agents today
Why "embedded human judgment" is critical for trust and governance
Agentic transactions vs. OpenRTB
Why advertising should behave more like portfolio management than day trading
The future of media mix modeling in an AI-first world
Which advertising jobs disappear, and which become even more valuable
