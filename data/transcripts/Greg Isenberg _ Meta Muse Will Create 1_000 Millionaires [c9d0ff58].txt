Numbered Section Summaries

1. The Night Nurse Search 

My wife and I recently had a baby, and our search for a night nurse led us to an agency. The agency admitted it searched Facebook groups for Miami, South Florida, and New York night nurses, and I realized Muse can do that work today. I expect a thousand-plus millionaires to come out of this era.

2. Matching Buyers and Sellers 

Billions of people post needs and offers in Facebook groups, on Marketplace, and on Instagram. I pick a niche, a city, and rules for who I approve, like night nurses in Orlando, and Muse handles the structure, matching, DMs, calls, texts, and booking. The top eBay sellers made millions by finding unique products to sell, and connecting buyer and seller holds the same value here.

3. Clean Data as an Asset 

Posts across these platforms vary in quality, so Muse cleans the data into Airtable, Google Sheets, or whatever I use. That list of names, needs, prices, and trust signals compounds into an asset I own and can sell, and I'll cover data brokering in a future episode. Each niche needs its own cleaning approach, starting with what my ICP wants.

4. A Phone Line and Undervalued Pages 

People give Muse a dedicated number through Bland AI's Agent Line, a non-VoIP US number with talk and text that I believe costs about $15, so Muse can get a number, call me, reach out to people, and close sales. Facebook groups have about 1.8 billion monthly active users by my estimate, and with millions of Muse agents crawling them, I'd start a group, a business page like a Miami agency for night nurses, or an IG niche page now. Buying Facebook groups may also be an arbitrage, so check Facebook's terms of service first.

5. The Four Levels 

Level one is mining other people's groups, Marketplace, and IG, and level two is owning a cleaned, priced, trusted list off platform. Level three is owning the room through your own Facebook group, IG niche page, or Marketplace storefront, and level four is building technology like SaaS, vertical agents, and vertical harnesses that automate the workflow end to end. Muse also works as my content hub for Instagram research, and its lean cost structure lets me charge parents a few hundred dollars where an agency team might have charged thousands.
