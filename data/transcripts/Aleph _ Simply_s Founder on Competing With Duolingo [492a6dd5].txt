[SHOW NOTES]
Yuval Kaminka, founder and CEO of Simply, on how he thinks about competitors like Duolingo moving into music.

When Michael Eisenberg asks whether Duolingo giving away music worries him, Yuval says he's aware of it and watches competitors all the time. "We learn from them, and we know they learn a lot from us." His case for why a rival is useful: "Having a competitor helps us define how we're going to be better. If you're lacking that, you need to disrupt yourself all the time."
