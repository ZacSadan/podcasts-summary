On-device voice AI runs speech recognition, text-to-speech, and dialogue entirely on edge hardware — no cloud, no internet, no per-call fees. This demo shows a conversational voice assistant that responds in 0.3–0.5 seconds, works fully offline, and keeps all voice data on-premises.

What is on-device voice AI? It is voice processing that happens on the device itself instead of sending audio to a cloud server. The result: half-second response times, privacy by design, and deployments that work even when the network cannot be trusted — in care facilities, at service kiosks, on factory floors, and in smart homes.
