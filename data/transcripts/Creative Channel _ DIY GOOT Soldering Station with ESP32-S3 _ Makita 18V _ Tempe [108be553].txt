[SHOW NOTES]
In this video, I build a complete KST GOOT Soldering Station using an ESP32-S3, a color display, real-time temperature sensing, and an 18V Makita LXT battery.

In the past, I used GOOT soldering tips in a very basic way. I only used the two heater terminals together with a quick-charge trigger board and a push button, which meant I basically had to guess the tip temperature.

However, these GOOT soldering tips also have two additional K-type thermocouple terminals for temperature sensing.

So I decided to take full advantage of the tip and build a proper temperature-controlled soldering station.

🌐 Free firmware + wiring diagrams + instructions:
https://ai.kenhsangtao.com/goot/

🧊 3D-printable enclosure:
https://makerworld.com/en/models/3255627-diy-goot-soldering-station-with-esp32-s3#profileId-3690114

Main hardware:
- ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 N16R8
- 1.47-inch ST7789 SPI TFT display, 172×320 resolution
- MAX31855 K-type thermocouple temperature sensor module
- EC11 rotary encoder module with push button
- IRLB8721 N-channel power MOSFET
- MP1584 DC-DC step-down converter for the 5V supply
- 5V active buzzer
- Roller lever limit switch for iron-stand detection
- Makita LXT 18–21V battery

Main features:
- Real-time soldering-tip temperature monitoring
- Encoder temperature adjustment
- ±5°C per encoder step
- Quick temperature presets:
300 / 330 / 360 / 390 / 420 / 450 / 480 / 500°C
- Placing the iron on the stand immediately activates Sleep mode at 50°C
- Lifting the iron restores the previous temperature setpoint
- Sleep mode can be enabled or disabled in Settings
- Audible confirmation beeps
- Built-in Wi-Fi settings page
- °C/°F selection
- Display brightness adjustment
- Buzzer settings
- Temperature offset calibration
