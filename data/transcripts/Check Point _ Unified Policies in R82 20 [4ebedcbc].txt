Imagine enforcing consistent security policies across your entire organization with a single unified policy. Check Point R82.20 introduces Unified Policies, enabling administrators to manage traditional firewalls, Cloud Firewalls, SASE, and SD-WAN from one centralized policy framework. This simplifies policy management, reduces operational complexity, and helps ensure consistent security across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

In this video, We explain how Unified Policies work and demonstrates how a single policy can be applied across multiple security products. You will see how the Infinity Portal provides a centralized management experience, allowing security teams to define, manage, and enforce consistent access policies regardless of where users, applications, or workloads are located.

Whether you are managing on-premises firewalls, cloud environments, remote users, or branch offices, this feature provides a unified approach to policy administration while reducing configuration errors and improving operational efficiency.

Ready to discover how Unified Policies simplify enterprise security management with Check Point R82.20? Watch this video!
