✂️ **Why carry five different tools when one pair of scissors can do the job?**

Meet these **Japanese multifunctional scissors**—far more than an ordinary cutting tool.

Designed for everyday convenience, they feature angled handles for smoother cardboard cutting and textured blades to help prevent sticky tape residue.

But the real attraction is everything packed into the design: 🔍

🧲 Magnetic tip for picking up small metal objects.  
🏷️ Built-in label cutter.  
🍾 Bottle-cap ring remover.  
🔧 A dedicated can-piercing tool.

It's practically a miniature toolbox disguised as a pair of scissors!

But here's the patent catch... 👀

PatentYogi Search Experts found a **2009 U.S. patent** describing multifunction scissors that already combined angled handles with several built-in tools.

So, despite the clever collection of features, the fundamental multi-tool scissors concept had already been disclosed.
