Understand Adaptive IPS logging, monitoring, and operational visibility in Check Point R82.10.

In this final episode of the Adaptive IPS video series, Check Point's Technical Marketing Engineering Team demonstrates how Adaptive IPS events appear in logs and how administrators can monitor feature activity in both Detect and Prevent modes.
