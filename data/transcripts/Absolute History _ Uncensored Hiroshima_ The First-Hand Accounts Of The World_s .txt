This premium documentary explores the devastating reality of the first atomic bomb dropped on Hiroshima on August 6, 1945. Through rare, deeply moving firsthand accounts from survivors, medical personnel, and the crew of the Enola Gay, we uncover the immediate horrors of the blast—from the 4,000°C heatwave to the toxic radioactive black rain. We also examine the Nagasaki bombing, the eventual surrender of Imperial Japan, and the haunting legacy of radiation sickness that changed global warfare forever.
