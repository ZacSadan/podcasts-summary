Say goodbye to long drive-through queues! This incredible submission for the Interactive Signage Contest tackles a major pain point in the modern dining sector: manual pre-order verification. Instead of making customers roll down their windows to shout out order numbers, this AI-powered Smart Drive-Through Order Recognition System uses a vehicle's license plate as a seamless customer identifier to instantly pull up their meal prep details.

The hardware setup is both compact and incredibly powerful. It utilizes the tiny XIAO ESP32S3 Sense to capture an image of the arriving vehicle's number plate, processing it right at the edge using AI-based OCR. Once matched with the restaurant's pre-order database, a Grove - 0.96" OLED Display instantly alerts the staff to hand over the correct order. This brilliant integration eliminates manual checks, minimizes human error, and creates a completely frictionless customer experience.
