Jack Dorsey’s company, Block, recently launched Buzz. It's basically a Slack workspace but for AI agents, and it's currently free, decentralized and open-source.

Here's how it works:
👋 It starts you off with a core team: Fizz (the builder), Honey (the writer), and Bumble (the researcher).
💻 You can give them parallel tasks and watch them build it in real time using any model you choose.
✍️ You can even tag them in a thread to peer-review each other's code and offer constructive feedback.
🔒 Because it's built on the open-source Nostr protocol, it's model-agnostic, decentralized, and entirely under your control.

This kind of alternative is great for developers, startups, and anyone looking to run a self-sovereign AI workforce under their own control.
