FLEXCORE – Gamified Joint Rehabilitation: An Interactive Approach for Neuromuscular Patients

FLEXCORE is a smart rehabilitation system developed to support neuromuscular patients through interactive, game-based therapy. The project transforms conventional rehabilitation exercises into an engaging experience by combining wearable sensor technology, virtual reality (VR), and real-time feedback.

The system uses a rehabilitation glove equipped with sensors to detect wrist movements such as flexion, extension, adduction, and abduction, along with finger-pressure inputs. These movements are mapped to game and VR environments, enabling patients to perform therapeutic exercises while controlling on-screen activities. This approach increases patient motivation, participation, and adherence to rehabilitation programs.

FLEXCORE incorporates Seeed Studio Grove sensors for reliable sensing and easy hardware integration. Sensor data is processed by a microcontroller and transmitted to a computer for real-time monitoring, performance analysis, and progress tracking. The platform supports individualized therapy sessions, data logging, and rehabilitation reports that can assist therapists in evaluating patient improvement.

By integrating gamification, VR-based training, and wearable sensing technology, FLEXCORE offers an affordable and accessible rehabilitation solution for individuals with neuromuscular disorders, helping improve motor function, coordination, and overall therapy outcomes.
