[SHOW NOTES]
Daniel Lubetzky on what Nuseir Yassin of Nas Daily told him about Judaism, and why it's a builder's superpower.

The two are close friends and frequent collaborators, and Nuseir pointed out something about Daniel's own religion that Daniel hadn't considered: in Judaism, people are remarkably comfortable questioning things. Daniel thinks that's the whole game — not just for Jews, but for builders in any religion. People who are at ease asking why can get to why not, and then to how this could be done better.

Which leads to the harder question. Is resolving the Israeli-Palestinian conflict daunting? Yes. Are these conflicts deep and entrenched? Also yes. His answer isn't optimism, it's assignment: "All it takes is every one of us recognizing that we cannot be just watching the stuff, and become protagonists."
