🚲 **What if your bicycle felt more like a lounge chair?**

Meet the **recumbent bicycle**—a completely different take on the traditional riding position. Instead of sitting upright, the rider sits low with their back supported and their legs extended forward toward the pedals. 😌

It looks unusual, but the concept has been around for decades.

We put this specific bicycle design under the patent microscope 🔍 and found a **1997 U.S. patent** describing key features including a reclining seat, forward-mounted pedals, and an adjustable frame.

So while the riding experience may feel radically different, the core design isn't exactly new.

🎥 Watch the video to see why this comfortable two-wheeler scored low for novelty and patentability.
