[SHOW NOTES]
The project is about a smart sign board built based on a web interface which uses a seeed XIAO ESP32S3 module for controlling the brightness and operation of the signboard via wifi, based on motion detection by a Grove PIR motion Sensor and the ambient light level of the surroundings. The signboard can be monitored and controlled remotely, making it energy‑efficient and interactive.
