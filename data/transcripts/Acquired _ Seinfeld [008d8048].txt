Coca-Cola, which has owned Columbia Pictures since 1982, puts in $30M for 40%, and Columbia signs on to distribute Castle Rock’s films. 1988 Jerry Seinfeld and Larry David pitch NBC a one-off special, working title Seinfeld’s Stand-Up Diary. Sony buys Columbia Pictures, and with it the Castle Rock stake.
