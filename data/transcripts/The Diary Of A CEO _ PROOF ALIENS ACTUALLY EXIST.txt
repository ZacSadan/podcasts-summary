[SHOW NOTES]
Are you ready to learn if aliens REALLY exist?

Because right now, so many of us are caught between UFO files, AI panic, Mars colonisation and the very human question of whether we are completely alone…

It is one of the questions Neil deGrasse Tyson is asked again and again.

Neil is one of the world’s most recognised astrophysicists. He runs the Hayden Planetarium, hosts StarTalk and has spent his life making the biggest questions in science feel understandable.

This is his second time on the show. I told Neil something during our conversation that sums up exactly why I wanted to speak again: he's improved the lives of arguably hundreds of millions of people - he has this ability to take the most extraordinary questions seriously without making extraordinary claims too easily.

When he first came on the show, people didn't only learn the science - Neil took these massive, terrifying concepts like black holes, simulation theory and the cosmic perspective and made them make sense. 

This time we spoke about:

- Why humans are so obsessed with aliens.
- Whether intelligent life probably exists somewhere else.
- What the UFO footage really shows.
- How unclear evidence is not proof.
- What aliens might think of us.
- Will going to Mars actually save humanity?
- Is AI an existential threat?
- Why the question of God keeps appearing whenever we talk about the universe.

I told Neil that watching *Cosmos* was one of the things that made me more curious about aliens, meaning, purpose and God. It made me feel small, but in a way that made me want to understand more.

Then he said something that completely reframed it for me:

“Not only are we alive in this universe, the universe is alive within us.”

That's what a conversation with Neil does - it opens your mind in a direction you didn't expect.

Near the end, I told him that the impact he has had on my life can be summed up in one word: curiosity. Expanding my mind through which I see the world.

SO, for anyone curious about what is real, what is possible and what we might be getting wrong about the universe, I really recommend watching/listening to this conversation.
