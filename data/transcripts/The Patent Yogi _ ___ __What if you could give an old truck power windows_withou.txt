🪟⚙️ **What if you could give an old truck power windows—without replacing the door?**

This invention takes a manual window crank and adds a removable motorized system on top of it.

A 3D-printed mechanism grips the existing crank, while a silicone hand acts as the interface. Press a button, and the window moves up or down automatically. 🔧🤖

No new door.
No major modification.
Just a clever way to automate something that was already there.

🎥 **Watch the video to see this surprisingly simple power-window retrofit in action.**
