[SHOW NOTES]
The biggest threat to bees might be the hive itself, says Beewise founder Saar Safra.

A traditional hive is a sealed box that gives a beekeeper no way to act in real time — someone has to travel to the field, open every box, diagnose what's wrong, and apply a treatment by hand. Across millions of bees, that's physically impossible to do in time. Beewise replaced it with thousands of solar-powered devices running 24/7 from California to Florida, where AI identifies the problem and programs a robot to treat it on the spot.

"Even if you put a thousand people around a million bees, we would not be able to tell every single colony what's going on. The model does it in milliseconds."
