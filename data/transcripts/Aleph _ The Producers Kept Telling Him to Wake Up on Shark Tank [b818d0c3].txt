[SHOW NOTES]
Daniel Lubetzky on why his first season as a Shark Tank shark was much harder than it looks on TV.

He does the show to give back — teaching entrepreneurs the way he was taught — and he genuinely enjoys being on television. What he didn't expect was the room. The stage is enormous and it swallows all the energy in it, so Daniel would come off a segment convinced he'd been shouting over everyone, and get a producer in his ear telling him to step in, wake up, do something.

His own assessment, delivered to Michael Eisenberg: nobody had ever accused him of not talking enough. Until his first season on Shark Tank.
