💡 **You have the invention. You don’t have a huge patent budget. What now?**

Meet the **provisional patent application**—a way to establish a U.S. filing date with a lower initial filing investment than a nonprovisional application. 📄

It doesn’t get examined for patentability, but it can let you use the **“Patent Pending”** notice and gives you up to **12 months** to decide whether to move forward with a corresponding nonprovisional application. ⏳

That makes it especially useful during the early stage of a product—when you’re still developing prototypes, testing the market, talking to potential partners, or figuring out whether the idea has commercial potential. 🚀

But there’s an important detail: a provisional application **does not itself become a patent**, and the later nonprovisional application needs adequate support from what was disclosed in the provisional. 🔍
