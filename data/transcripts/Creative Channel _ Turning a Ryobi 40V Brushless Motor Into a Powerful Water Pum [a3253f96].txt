[SHOW NOTES]
In this project, I reused a Ryobi 40V brushless motor from a grass trimmer/brush cutter , rated at approximately 1500W and weighing around 1.8 kg , to build a powerful portable water pump.

Water Pump Kit
https://s.shopee.vn/9pdojRO0Ri

- The pump is driven by an approximately 1500W BLDC motor controller  and powered from a Makita 40V battery through a battery adapter.

- For better alignment and a cleaner installation, I designed a custom 3D printed mounting frame to hold the motor and aluminum water pump securely on the same base.

The result is a compact cordless water pump that can be powered by a 40V tool battery and easily carried wherever water pumping is needed.

- Custom designed 3D printed motor/pump mounting frame
https://makerworld.com/en/models/3221263-diy-1500w-cordless-water-pump-ryobi-40v-motor#profileId-3647517

⚠️ Always prime the pump with water before operation and avoid running the pump dry for extended periods.
