[SHOW NOTES]
Infrastructure cost visibility breaks down when spend lives outside the cloud bill. Jupiter closed a 20% to 40% gap with DoiT DataHub.

Jupiter, a crypto company on Solana, runs primarily on AWS, with acquired workloads on Cloudflare Workers, GCP and bare metal, and a large piece of its infrastructure spend on RPC providers that bill by invoice.

• The problem: invoices from RPC providers and other vendors were paid outside any cost system, leaving 20% to 40% of spend unaccounted for.
• What changed: DoiT support built the first ingestion pipeline. Invoices forwarded to a Google Drive folder are converted to DataHub format and ingested into DoiT Cloud Intelligence™.
• The result: 26 outside vendors now sit beside the cloud provider billing, with alerts, budgets, reports and forecasting across all of it.

"It's a massive unlock to get from 60% to 100% into one system, and DataHub is a great tool that is flexible and really lets us fill the gaps, but leverage all of the reporting."
Kyle Espinola, Head of Platform Engineering, Jupiter
