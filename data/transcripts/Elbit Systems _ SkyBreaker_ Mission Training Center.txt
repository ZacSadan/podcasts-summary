What if pilots could train for the most demanding missions without leaving the ground?

SkyBreaker™ is Elbit Systems' Fighter Aircraft Mission Training Center, providing a realistic environment for tactical training, mission rehearsal and operational preparation. From mission planning and briefing to execution and debriefing, aircrews can train together in complex scenarios that replicate modern combat conditions.

Designed to support multiple aircraft types, weapons and operational doctrines, SkyBreaker helps air forces improve readiness, refine tactics and prepare for missions while significantly reducing the need for actual flight hours.

Train as you fight. Plan, execute, evaluate and adapt. All within a single mission training environment.
