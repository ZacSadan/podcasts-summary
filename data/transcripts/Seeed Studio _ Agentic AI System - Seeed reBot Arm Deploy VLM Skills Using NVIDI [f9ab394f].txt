At WRC 2026 (World Robot Conference), Seeed is showcasing reBot B601-RS arm × VLM Skills, turning a robotic arm into an agentic Physical AI system.
Instead of following predefined programs, the VLM can dynamically:
Perceive → Reason → Plan → Act → Observe → Recover

Powered by NVIDIA Cosmos 3-Edge and 21 native Action Skills, reBot arm can adapt to real-world changes, recover from failures, and execute tasks through continuous closed-loop interaction.

The project was developed by Seeed team Wenhan Fan, YaoHui Zhu, youjiang yu, Junjie Liu, Vector Li, and Elaine Wu; together with NVIDIA team Asier Arranz, Johnny Núñez Cano, Minto Zheng, and Abhilash Somasamudramath. Appreciate everyone's great effort and contribution!❤️
