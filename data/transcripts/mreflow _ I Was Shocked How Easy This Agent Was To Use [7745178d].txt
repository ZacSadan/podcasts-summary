Is this the easiest AI agent to use?

I connected Meta's new AI Agent Muse to my email and asked it to audit my subscriptions to see how much I’m spending on AI tools. 

But what impressed me most wasn’t just the audit, it was how easy the agent was to use. It feels like a normal messaging app, runs tasks inside a secure virtual machine, and suggests ways it can help as you connect your apps.

If you’ve only used AI for chatting and haven’t tried agents yet, this might be one of the easiest places to start.
