[SHOW NOTES]
Are you waiting for the perfect circumstances to pursue greatness, or using your past as an excuse to avoid hard work? Legendary comedian Katt Williams reveals the story of his childhood and the mindset that turned a 13-year-old runaway into an entertainment icon.

Katt Williams is an Emmy Award-winning stand-up comedian, actor, and writer, widely regarded as one of the most prolific and distinctive voices in modern comedy. Beyond his massive success on stage and screen, he is the author of a deeply revealing memoir detailing his extraordinary ascent from homelessness.

*In this episode, he explains:*
■ *The Medicine of Humor:* How discovering comedy provided an immediate escape from severe childhood abuse and instilled an unbreakable sense of internal control.
■ *The Hero’s Delusion:* Why reading classic literature convinced him that extreme hardship was merely a prerequisite for greatness, bulletproofing his mind against a victim mentality.
■ *The Math of Rejection:* Why treating rejection as a predictable numbers game completely erases the emotional pain of failure in both business and comedy.
■ *The Illusion of "Overnight" Success:* Why treating comedy like a grueling 9 to 5 job for 15 unrecognized years set the foundation to dominate both Black and white audiences.
■ *The Ultimate Self-Bet:* How funding his own early comedy specials removed all career risk and proved that relying on industry gatekeepers is the only true gamble.
