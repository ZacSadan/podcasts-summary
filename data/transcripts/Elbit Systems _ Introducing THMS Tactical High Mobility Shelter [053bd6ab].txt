Modern land forces have to stay connected while constantly on the move, often far from any fixed infrastructure or static command post. THMS keeps them linked.

The Tactical High Mobility Shelter (THMS), developed and built by Elbit Systems Sweden, is a highly mobile, rapidly deployable battlefield networking platform for digitally connected and distributed land forces. Acting as a forward communication and sensing node, it extends both line-of-sight and beyond-line-of-sight (BLOS) connectivity so maneuvering units, sensors and command elements stay coordinated in complex and contested environments.

*Key capabilities:*
* Telescopic mast reaching up to 25 meters, supporting payloads up to 100 kg
* Multi-role integration: tactical communications, electronic warfare (EW), EO/IR sensors, surveillance, SATCOM, tactical 5G and high-power laser (HPL)
* Fully self-contained with integrated power, autonomous for up to 24 hours and independent of the host vehicle
* Automated mast deployment and a unique automatic guying rope system for setup in under five minutes
* Compatible with both tracked and wheeled platforms
* Developed and manufactured in Sweden with a European supply chain, built for extreme weather and Arctic conditions
