[SHOW NOTES]
For decades, personal protective equipment has relied on the same basic principle: create a barrier between people and potential threats. While effective, traditional gear can severely restrict mobility and cause heat stress, forcing operators in high-threat environments to choose between protection and operational effectiveness.

DARPA performer Singletto is changing that paradigm. Born from DARPA's Personalized Protective Biosystem (PPB) program, Singletto's Oxafence technology uses a photosensitizing dye to actively neutralize pathogens on contact, turning everyday materials into self-disinfecting gear.

DARPA’s Embedded Entrepreneur Initiative (EEI) is helping Singletto bridge the gap from lab to market, accelerating the transition of this game-changing technology into both defense and commercial applications.
