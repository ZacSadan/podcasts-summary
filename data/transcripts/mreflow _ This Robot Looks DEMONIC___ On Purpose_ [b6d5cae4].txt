[SHOW NOTES]
This robot looks straight up like a fantasy movie villain, but it was designed like that on purpose.

Despite how it looks, the Threehalves robot's design actually makes perfect sense: four legs for stability, tools that attach directly to its wrists, and horns that house cameras for a remote operator.

The goal is to let skilled workers tackle dangerous jobs from a safe distance, like wildfire zones, collapsed buildings, and toxic environments.

It’s still a half-built proof of concept. But this is the kind of robotics idea I want to see work. Even if I’d absolutely run the other way if I saw it in the woods.
