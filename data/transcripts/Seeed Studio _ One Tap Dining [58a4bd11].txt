[SHOW NOTES]
Our project, One-Tap Dining, is a smart restaurant service signaling system that allows customers to request assistance with a single button press. We use the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C3 to process the request, a Grove Button for customer input, and a Grove Chainable RGB LED to indicate service status. The alert is wirelessly sent to a Seeed Studio Wio Terminal, which acts as the waiter’s dashboard to display the table number and service request.
