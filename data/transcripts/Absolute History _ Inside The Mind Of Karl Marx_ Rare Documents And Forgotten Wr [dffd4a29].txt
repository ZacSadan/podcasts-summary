His theories sparked global revolutions, fueled the Cold War, and built powerful regimes from the Soviet Union to modern China. But was he a visionary prophet or the architect of history's deadliest ideological experiment? This documentary examines the life, radical ideas, and complex legacy of the man whose philosophy redefined world politics and economic thought forever.
