[SHOW NOTES]
CraveGuide is a voice-enabled smart restaurant assistant that helps customers discover and order food based on their cravings. Users simply speak their preference, such as spicy, sweet, sour, vegetarian, or non-vegetarian, and the system converts the speech into text, recommends suitable dishes, and allows users to navigate the menu, select quantity, and place an order.

The project incorporates Seeed Studio products at its core. The XIAO ESP32-S3-based EE04 E-Paper Display Driver controls the 4.26-inch 800×480 e-paper interface, providing a low-power and highly readable user interface. The Seeed Studio reSpeaker Flex Linear 4-Mic Array, with its onboard XMOS XVF3800, is used for voice capture and audio processing through I²S. The two modules communicate through UART, while ElevenLabs Speech-to-Text converts the recorded voice into text for the recommendation system.
