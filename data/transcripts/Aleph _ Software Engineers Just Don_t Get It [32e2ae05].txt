You can't 10x a chip company's velocity the way you can a software startup. The atoms don't move faster.
Elad Raz breaks down why: nine months are locked in manufacturing at TSMC before you see a single wafer. The design phase has its own hard constraints — verification, analog layout, place and route — all dominated by a small number of EDA tool vendors (Synopsys, Cadence, Mentor) who have no incentive to disrupt themselves. And a single mistake costs $50–$150 million to fix.
AI can improve what happens inside that design window. It cannot compress the physics of fabrication.
