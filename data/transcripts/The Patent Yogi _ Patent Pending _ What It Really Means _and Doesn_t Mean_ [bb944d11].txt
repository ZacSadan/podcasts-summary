📦🔒 **That tiny “Patent Pending” label can make a product look a lot more protected than it actually is.**

You’ve probably seen it on **tech products, packaging, and everyday gadgets**—but what does it really mean?

“Patent Pending” signals that a patent application has been filed. It can communicate that a company is claiming a new invention, but it **doesn’t mean the patent has already been granted**. 👀

That difference matters.

A pending application may give a company a strategic advantage and signal competitors to pay attention, but the rights that come with an issued patent are different from simply having an application on file. 🔍

🎥 **Watch the video** to understand what “Patent Pending” really means—and why those two words shouldn’t be confused with full patent protection.
