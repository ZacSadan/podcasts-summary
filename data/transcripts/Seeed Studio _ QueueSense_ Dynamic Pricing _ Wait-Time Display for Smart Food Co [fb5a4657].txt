[SHOW NOTES]
QueueSense uses the Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 and EE04 e-Paper board to build a smart queue and dynamic pricing display for busy food courts. A proximity sensor detects crowd density, and the e-Paper screen shows real-time wait times and adaptive pricing. Staff can adjust settings via onboard buttons, while Wi-Fi enables live menu synchronization. The EE04's ultra-low power consumption and battery support allow flexible, wire-free installation. This project transforms static signage into an interactive, data-driven tool that improves customer flow and operational transparency.
