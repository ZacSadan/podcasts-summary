Most honey sold in the US isn't real honey, and Beewise founder Saar Safra has a name for it: honey laundering.

Cheap rice and corn syrup gets dumped onto the market with the same color and texture as honey, then sold as the real thing for a fraction of what it costs to produce. The shopper can't tell the difference. Their body can.

"Only 10% of it is real honey. 90% of it is a sweetener that just does harm to your body."

It's the kind of detail that reframes an entire grocery aisle.
