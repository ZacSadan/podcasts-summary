[SHOW NOTES]
Yuval Ariav explains how JIGA tackles custom hardware procurement, a global market worth roughly $1.1 trillion.

When a company needs hardware that isn't off-the-shelf, someone has to run a long, painful procurement process — and that someone is usually an engineer. As Yuval puts it, they become "procurement bureaucrats." JIGA takes it over end to end: running the awarding process, vetting suppliers, checking certifications, pulling quotes, and making sure delivery and fulfillment actually happen. Yuval pegs the worldwide spend on this kind of custom hardware procurement at around $1.1 trillion in 2025 — and believes JIGA's founders could become role models for the next generation of Israeli builders. "They become procurement bureaucrats. They don't like that."
