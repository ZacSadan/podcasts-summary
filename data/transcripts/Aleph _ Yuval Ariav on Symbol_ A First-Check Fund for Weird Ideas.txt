[SHOW NOTES]
Yuval Ariav breaks down how Symbol invests: a concentrated portfolio, high ownership, and a bet on non-consensus Israeli founders.

Symbol is built to write the first check into the ideas other funds find too strange — the non-consensus, pre-consensus opportunities in Israeli tech. Yuval lays out the model: 16 to 18 investments, leading or co-leading most of them, buying meaningful ownership upfront, around $2M average initial check. His conviction is that in Israel, smaller portfolios with higher ownership pay off better than spreading capital thin. "We want to make Symbol into the go-to first-check fund for the weird Israeli venture opportunities."
