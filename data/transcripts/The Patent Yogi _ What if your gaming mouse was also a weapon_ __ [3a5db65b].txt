What if your gaming mouse was also a weapon? 🎮🔫

This product takes the familiar gaming mouse and gives it a completely different form factor: a **gun-shaped body with a trigger, aiming-style controls, and an optical sensor.**

The goal is obvious—make shooting games feel more immersive and physical. 🎯

At first glance, it feels like one of those products that could instantly grab gamers’ attention:

🔥 Eye-catching design
🎮 Built around gaming behavior
🎯 More physical interaction
⚡ Designed for a more realistic experience

But there’s a catch.

When a product feels *too* familiar, the question becomes: **how much of the core concept is actually new?** 🔍

That doesn’t mean the modern product is identical. But it does raise a serious patentability question.
