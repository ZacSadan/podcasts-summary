Welcome to Part 2 of my DIY AI Xiaozhi Wire Robot project!

In this video, I upgrade the robot by installing a TOF050C VL6180 Time-of-Flight distance sensor to detect edges and prevent the robot from falling off tables or other elevated surfaces.

This sensor measures distance in real time, allowing the robot to recognize dangerous edges and improve autonomous navigation.

In this video:
Soldering the TOF050C VL6180 sensor
Wiring the sensor to the ESP32-S3
Installing the anti-fall sensor on the robot
Testing edge detection
Preparing for future autonomous navigation features
