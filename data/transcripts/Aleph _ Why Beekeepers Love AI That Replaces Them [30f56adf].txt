Beewise built an AI-powered beehive that outperforms human beekeepers, so you'd expect beekeepers to resent it. They're its biggest fans instead.

Saar Safra's explanation: Beewise isn't replacing the beekeeper, it's a technology company that makes the box. A commercial beekeeper running 10,000 colonies partners with Beewise, and together they bring the same bees to growers like Olam and Driscoll's. The payoff is one number — colony collapse drops from the industry's 30–60% to under 8%.

"Today we have the best beekeeper on the planet. It supersedes any human beekeeper you can imagine. It's just not human."
