🧥✈️ **This jacket is basically a travel bag you can wear.**

Meet the **Voyage Coat**—a travel-focused jacket with **16+ pockets** designed to carry clothes, your passport, phone, and other essentials without relying on a traditional bag. 🎒

But the smartest feature is what happens when you take it off.

The entire jacket can **fold into its own built-in duffel bag**, turning your wearable luggage into an actual travel bag. 🤯

Sounds incredibly clever—but has someone already thought of it? 👀

PatentYogi Search Experts found a **2017 U.S. patent application** describing a garment designed to carry clothing and personal belongings before folding into a carry-on bag.

That’s remarkably close to the core concept behind the Voyage Coat. 🔍

So while the product may have its own design and features, the **wearable garment-to-travel-bag concept** was already disclosed.
