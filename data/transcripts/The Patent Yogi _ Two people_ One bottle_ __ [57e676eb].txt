Two people. One bottle. 🥤🥤

This unusual bottle skips the traditional base and adds an opening at both ends—so two people can drink from it at the same time.

It could be a surprisingly practical idea for sharing drinks while keeping things simple and convenient.

But there’s one problem: **someone thought of the concept decades ago.** 👀

So would you still turn this into a product?

Watch the video to see why this simple bottle scored low on novelty but high on commercial value.
