🔌📱 What if your charger knew exactly when to let go?

Meet this self-ejecting phone charger—a charging cable designed to automatically disconnect from your phone once the battery reaches 100%. ⚡

The clever part is mechanical.

When the phone is fully charged, two small pins near the connector extend outward and physically push the connector out of the charging port. No reaching for the cable. No manual unplugging. 😮

But here’s the patent catch... 🔍

PatentYogi Search Experts found a 2020 U.S. patent application describing an auto-eject charging cord using battery monitoring and mechanical pins to eject the connector when charging is complete.

That’s remarkably close to this product’s core mechanism.

🎥 Watch the video to see why this charger scored 7/10 for commercial value, but only 2/10 for novelty and patentability.
