This week’s Paper Club is all about AI for biology. The cost of sequencing has collapsed, which means biology now generates more data than anyone can mine, and the models are finally catching up. We cover how falling sequencing costs are turning cancer care into a per-patient data problem, what it takes to model a cell rather than a molecule, what a language model trained on DNA actually learns, and why in-context learning showed up in a genome model that nobody trained for it.
