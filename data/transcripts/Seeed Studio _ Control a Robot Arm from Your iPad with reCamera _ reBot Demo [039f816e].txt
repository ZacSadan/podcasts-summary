Control a reBot robotic arm from an iPad with reCamera. This browser-based teleoperation demo combines live camera streaming and onboard arm control—no external PC needed for arm control.

Inspired by Gold Miner, we used the tablet interface to manually position the gripper, lift a plush toy, and drop a real prize.

Mounted on the arm, reCamera streams the live view and runs the armd control software. The B4 CAN board connects it to reBot B601-DM, turning touchscreen commands into physical movement.
