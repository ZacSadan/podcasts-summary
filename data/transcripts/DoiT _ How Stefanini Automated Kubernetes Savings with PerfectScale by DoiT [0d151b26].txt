Kubernetes cost optimization starts with seeing the waste. Stefanini's FinOps team
found 26% in monthly compute savings with PerfectScale by DoiT.

https://doit.com

Stefanini is a global technology consulting firm delivering IT service desk, ITO and
BPO for its customers on a Kubernetes-based platform.

- The problem: Kubernetes cost data existed, but it was hard to visualize which
  workloads were driving spend, and engineers were copying resource requests between
  deployments instead of sizing CPU and memory to the job.
- What changed: PerfectScale exposed per-workload efficiency inside the clusters, so
  sizing problems were caught before production. Automation then applied the
  right-sizing continuously.
- The result: about 26% of total compute saved every month, with the platform team
  freed up for strategic work instead of manual tuning.

"Without the tool, I don't know how we would do it. We'd have to have twice the
number of people on my team to stay on top of it, but with PerfectScale, we can
focus on the more strategic issues."
David Adkins, Director of Software & Platform Engineering, Stefanini
