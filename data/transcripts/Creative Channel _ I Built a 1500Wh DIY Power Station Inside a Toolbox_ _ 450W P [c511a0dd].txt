I BUILT A 1500Wh DIY POWER STATION INSIDE A TOOLBOX!

Hey everyone! Welcome back to another DIY project!

In today's video, I'm turning an ordinary plastic toolbox into a portable battery power station using 18 high-capacity LiPo cells and a 450W pure sine wave inverter.

The battery pack consists of 18 LiPo cells rated at 3.7V and 20Ah each, arranged in a 3S6P configuration to provide a total capacity of 120Ah.

The battery stores approximately 1,332Wh of nominal energy, or 1,512Wh when calculated using its fully charged voltage of 12.6V.

I also installed two 220V AC outlets, dual USB-C PD charging ports, a USB QC 3.0 port, a 12V automotive socket, an XT60 connector, and digital power monitoring displays.

Everything fits neatly inside a portable toolbox, making this a compact DIY power station for various off-grid and backup power applications.

⚡ DIY POWER STATION SPECIFICATIONS:

- Battery: 18 × 3.7V 20Ah LiPo cells
- Battery configuration: 3S6P
- Nominal voltage: 11.1V
- Fully charged voltage: 12.6V
- Battery capacity: 120Ah
- Nominal energy capacity: 1,332Wh
- Capacity calculated at full voltage: 1,512Wh
- Inverter: 450W pure sine wave, 12V DC to 220V AC
- Battery protection: 3S 200A BMS
- AC outputs: 2 × 220V outlets
- USB charging: 2 × USB-C PD + 1 × USB QC 3.0
- DC outputs: 12V automotive socket and XT60 connector
- Monitoring: Digital AC power meter and battery capacity display
- Enclosure: Portable plastic toolbox

🔧 MAIN COMPONENTS:
- Plastic toolbox
- 18 high-capacity LiPo battery cells
- 450W pure sine wave inverter
- 3S 200A battery management system
- Digital AC power meter
- Dual USB-C PD and USB QC 3.0 charger
- 220V AC outlets
- 12V cigarette lighter socket
- XT60 panel connector
- Battery capacity indicator
- ON/OFF power switch

⚠️ SAFETY NOTE:
This project involves high-capacity lithium batteries and potentially dangerous 220V AC power. Proper battery protection, fuses, wiring, insulation, and electrical safety precautions are essential.
