Giving feedback is a delicate art. Come in too hot, and you might nuke the other person’s confidence (or invite fiery retaliation). Come in too soft, and you might not make an impact.

To strike the right balance, executive coach Alisa Cohn suggests treating feedback as a process rather than a one-off course correction. Grow your relationship over time; offer specific, actionable advice when appropriate; and follow up as necessary.
