Flip phones emerged in the early 1990s, with the Motorola StarTAC (1996) becoming the first iconic flip device. The form factor exploded in popularity during the 2000s with models like the Motorola Razr, offering compact design and screen protection.

The appeal centered on portability and ease of use, though flip phones declined as larger touchscreen smartphones dominated the market. Even before Apple introducing the iPhone Duo, companies like Samsung and Motorola have revived flip phones using foldable screen technology, bringing back the nostalgic design with modern capabilities.
