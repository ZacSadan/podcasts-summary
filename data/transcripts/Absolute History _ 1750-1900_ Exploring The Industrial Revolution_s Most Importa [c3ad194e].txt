[SHOW NOTES]
From Big Pit's dark shafts to Canary Wharf's glass towers, Britain’s landscape tells a radical story. Fly above the clues carved by coal, cotton, steam and steel.

Join historian Dr Alex Langlands on a south Wales flight path to the valleys where open-cast scars and towering spoil heaps fueled a steam-powered leap forward. Meet retired miner Kerry Thompson as he recalls camaraderie 90 metres down and the dangers that shadowed every shift. Then track Isambard Kingdom Brunel’s ambition from the Clifton Suspension Bridge to the Great Western Railway, a vision that stitched mines, mills and ports together. In Manchester’s Cottonopolis, discover why rivers, damp air, and a freewheeling oligarchy supercharged textile mills run by women and children. Step into back-to-back housing, wake-week escapes to Blackpool Tower’s ballroom, and the precarious present at Port Talbot. Finally, see how derelict Docklands became Canary Wharf.

This is the Industrial Revolution written onto the land - and still shaping lives today. Watch to uncover the surprising links you can only see from above.
