Humanoid robots have arrived to New York City, and they're ready for purchase!

In this episode of NYC Innovates, we visit Koid Shop, NYC's first retail location dedicated to general-purpose humanoid robots. We meet the companies building the future: Catena's avatar control technology, Inspired's socially-aware robots with personality, and AGIBOTs AI-powered dancers. We discuss the price barriers, safety concerns, and the real-world applications transforming everything from airports to your home. The biggest questions remain: how will these robots do in the consumer market? and would you buy one?
