📐📱 **A patent drawing can communicate far more than words ever could.**

Think about a complex invention: gestures, moving parts, proportions, interfaces, or the way different components interact. A well-prepared patent drawing can make those relationships immediately understandable. 👀

That’s why patent drawings aren’t just illustrations added at the end of an application.

They can help **define the visual and structural details of an invention**, communicate how it works, and make important features easier to understand during examination or later disputes. 🔍

The Apple–Samsung patent battles are a powerful reminder of how much detail can matter when product design and functionality are being scrutinized. 🍎⚖️

🎥 **Watch the video** to see why treating patent drawings as “filler” can be a costly mistake—and why the best drawings can become part of your invention’s story.

**Your claims define the invention. Your drawings help bring it to life.** ✏️
