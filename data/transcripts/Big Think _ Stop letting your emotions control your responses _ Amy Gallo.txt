Philosopher Friedrich Nietzche is credited with saying, “One ought to hold on to one’s heart; for if one lets it go, one soon loses control of the head, too.” Similarly, workplace expert Amy Gallo argues that our emotions often hijack our rational thinking during a conflict, causing us to craft stories that may not be wholly based on reality yet still fuel our reactions.

In this video lesson, she discusses how practicing self-awareness and mindfulness can help you regain control over your inner narrative and maintain both your self-control and your relationships in the face of conflict.
