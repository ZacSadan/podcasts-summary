🚗💨 **Fresh air without opening the car window?**

This clever gadget turns your car’s own movement into an air-intake system.

Clip it onto the window, start driving, and the forward airflow gets captured and redirected into the cabin through a specially shaped tube. 🌬️

The smart part? A mesh helps keep bugs and road debris outside while letting air in. 🦟🚫

It’s a simple idea—but a fascinating example of how **everyday airflow can be turned into a useful mechanical system.**
