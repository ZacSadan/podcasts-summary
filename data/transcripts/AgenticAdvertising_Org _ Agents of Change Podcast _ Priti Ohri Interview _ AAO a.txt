[SHOW NOTES]
In the Agents of Change x AgenticAdvertising.org interview series, sponsored by Agnitio, Aubriana Alvarez Lopez sits down at Cannes Lions 2026 with Priti Ohri, CEO & Co-founder of Advertible. They discuss what agentic AI actually looks like inside a growing advertising business.

Rather than replacing people, Priti shares how her team is using AI agents to eliminate busy work, amplify creative at scale, and free humans to focus on higher-value strategic thinking. She also explains why trust and governance remain non-negotiable, how multiple agents verify each other's work, and why native advertising may be one of the best proving grounds for agentic workflows.

In this episode, we cover:

• How Advertible uses AI agents across marketing, engineering, and production
• Why every AI output should be verified—not blindly trusted
• Scaling creative without sacrificing brand governance
• Why native advertising is uniquely positioned for agentic AI
• The advertising jobs AI will eliminate—and the ones it will elevate
