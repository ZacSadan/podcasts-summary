🏡🌊 **Who needs a waterfront house when your entire home can float on the water?**

Meet **TinyTanic**—an eight-foot floating micro-home that takes compact living to an entirely different level.

Built on plastic pontoons, this tiny cabin combines the comforts of home with the freedom of a boat.

Inside, you'll find a bed, mini kitchen, sink, and storage. It even features a lake-water purification system, while solar panels and an electric motor help make it self-contained and mobile. ☀️⚡

But here's where this tiny floating home hits a patent challenge... 👀

🔍 PatentYogi Search Experts found a **2008 U.S. patent for a boat camper** that already combined pontoons, sleeping space, kitchen facilities, solar power, and electric propulsion.

That's remarkably close to TinyTanic's overall concept.

So while its compact design and practical features make it an interesting floating home, the fundamental idea had already been disclosed in earlier patent literature.
