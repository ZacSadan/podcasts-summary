📱 **A phone case designed to stop people from swiping.**

You hand someone your phone to take a picture—and suddenly they’re exploring your entire camera roll.

The Swiper Stopper takes a **hardware-first approach** to the problem.

Instead of an app or screen lock, the case itself can cover the display. A flexible panel swings over the screen and uses magnets to stay locked in place.

That creates a simple product proposition:

🔒 Control access physically
📱 Keep your phone fully functional
🧲 No software required
🛡️ Add a physical privacy barrier when needed

The interesting part? The concept isn’t just about privacy—it’s about turning a familiar annoyance into a **simple physical product solution.**
