But user data tells a different story. Among OpenRouter customers spending at least $100 a month, the median number of AI model families in use has doubled over the past year , from three to six. Even as AI models become more capable, businesses are using more models, not fewer. This makes sense when you consider that intelligence isn’t one-dimensional. A model that’s exceptional at writing code may not be the fastest at answering customer questions, or the most economical at processing millions of documents. The question is shifting from “which AI model should we build on?” to “which model should handle each task?” The answer, increasingly, is ‘it depends.’ Which is great for flexibility, less great for simplicity.
