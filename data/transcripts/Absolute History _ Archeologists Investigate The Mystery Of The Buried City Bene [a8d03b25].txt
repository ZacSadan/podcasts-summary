Petra guards more than sandstone splendor. Beneath Al-Khazneh, whispers of buried riches still echo.

Journalist Jamie Theakston gains rare access inside the Treasury, then descends into the tombs below where offerings once hung and royal burials lay. Beyond daylight, he follows Bedouin guides on a near-silent night dig, where a simple oil lamp surfaces from the dark. In Amman, a vast private collection reveals shelves of Petra oil lamps, ossuaries, Roman glass, copper scrolls, and even cuneiform tablets the owner dates to deep antiquity. So why do locals risk jail to dig? How much of Petra is still untouched? And where do the most valuable finds really end up?

From ingenious Nabataean waterworks to legends of Pharaoh’s lost hoard, this episode explores the city’s hidden economy of relics and the tantalizing fact that most of Petra remains unexcavated.
