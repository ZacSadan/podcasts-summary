🚗 Your car has mirrors—but can they really show you everything?

Meet Blind Spot Buddy 👀—a small magnetic camera designed to give drivers a live view of those hard-to-see areas around their vehicle.

Simply place the removable camera where you need it, and view the footage on a monitor inside the car. 📹

Sounds like a smart safety innovation, right? But when we looked into the existing technology, we found something interesting... 🔍

A similar earlier invention already disclosed the core combination of vehicle cameras, magnetic attachment, wireless transmission, and an in-car monitor.

So, does using just one removable camera instead of multiple cameras create enough of a difference? 🤔
