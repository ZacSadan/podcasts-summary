Beewise founder Saar Safra explains how a software-updated, solar-powered beehive cuts colony collapse to under 8%, against an industry norm of 30–60%.

What changes for the average person: cheaper, better food, and a real boost to biodiversity, since the bees pollinate every flower in a region, not just the crop they're placed on. The hives update weekly, over the air — bug fixes and improvements pushed like any other device. Because they run on solar, they can go almost anywhere, including the places that need them most, like Africa and South America.

"Not bee fixes, bug fixes."
