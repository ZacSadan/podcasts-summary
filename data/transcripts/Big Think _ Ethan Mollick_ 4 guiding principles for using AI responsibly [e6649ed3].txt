Lego sets famously come with detailed instructions, including parts lists and visual, step-by-step directions. But generative AI isn’t far enough along for that type of support. (You’re lucky if you get a few tooltips thrown in.) Working with such an open-ended technology is an exciting prospect, but it can also be difficult to feel confident as you do so.

Luckily, Wharton Professor Ethan Mollick has done some of the legwork upfront. In this video lesson, he explains how to invite AI into your workflow – and discusses important guardrails to make sure a human (such as yourself) remains “in the loop.”
