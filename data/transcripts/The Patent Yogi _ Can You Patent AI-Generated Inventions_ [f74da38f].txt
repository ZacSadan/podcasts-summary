🤖🦷 **You ask AI to invent a toothbrush. It gives you a breakthrough design. So… who owns the invention?**

This is becoming one of the most important questions in modern innovation.

Imagine using AI to generate a new toothbrush concept, test different mechanisms, and refine the design in minutes. The machine may contribute heavily to the process—but under current patent rules in the **U.S., Europe, and the UK, an AI system cannot be named as the inventor.** 👀

That doesn’t mean AI-assisted inventions are off-limits.

The key question is **what the human actually contributed**. If you made the inventive decisions, shaped the solution, and contributed to the conception of the invention, there may still be a path to patent protection. 🔍

So AI isn't necessarily replacing the inventor.

It could become the **most powerful tool in the inventor’s toolbox.** 🚀

🎥 **Watch the video** to see where the line between AI assistance and human inventorship gets really interesting.
