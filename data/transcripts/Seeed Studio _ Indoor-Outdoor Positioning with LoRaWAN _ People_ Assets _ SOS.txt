[SHOW NOTES]
Most facilities track people and assets with three separate systems — BLE for indoor, GPS for outdoor, cellular for fleet. Three gateways, three dashboards, three cellular bills. Integration cost often exceeds the hardware. This video shows how one LoRaWAN network replaces all three.

Full deployment topology, BOM configurator, and reference designs:
https://www.seeed.cc/solutions/indoor-outdoor-positioning

The configurator auto-matches hardware based on tracking target (personnel vs vehicle), coverage area (indoor vs outdoor), and frequency band per country.

Hardware:
$39.90 badge (T1000) — GNSS + BLE + WiFi + one-tap SOS with identity and zone reference
$12 BLE beacons (BC03) — room-level indoor positioning
$49.90 solar tracker (T2000-C) — vehicles and outdoor assets, no battery swaps
$99 LoRaWAN gateway (SenseCAP M2) — one gateway, all devices

The line item that kills TCO on positioning? Per-device cellular subscriptions. This architecture has none. Open LoRaWAN standard — no proprietary lock-in. Frequency bands configurable per deployment country (EU868, US915, AS923). ATEX-certified for oil, gas, and mining.
