[SHOW NOTES]
During the Cold War, the US military launched a top-secret quest to revolutionize battlefield mobility by putting individual soldiers in the sky. From dangerous platform rotors and flying jeeps to Bell's iconic hydrogen-peroxide rocket belt and jet-powered backpacks, engineers raced to create a lethal edge over communist forces. Featuring rarely-seen archival footage and declassified tests, this documentary uncovers the brilliant innovations, deadly crashes, and extreme risks behind America's secret flying soldier programs—and why the military ultimately abandoned personal jetpacks in favor of the legendary Huey helicopter.
