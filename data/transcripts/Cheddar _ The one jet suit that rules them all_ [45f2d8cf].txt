The Gravity jet suit emerged from prototyping that began in 2016, with the first untethered flight completed in November of that year. The company conducted its public launch in April 2017. 

Since then, the suit has undergone continuous refinement, with the current Mk4 model incorporating 3D-printed aluminum and titanium components. The suit costs $440,000 but remains available primarily through leasing arrangements rather than direct sales, with customers paying for training and flight time at Gravity's facilities.
