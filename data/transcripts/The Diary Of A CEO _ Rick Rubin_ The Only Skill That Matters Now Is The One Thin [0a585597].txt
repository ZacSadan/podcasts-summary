[SHOW NOTES]
Discover the spiritual and psychological creative process that legendary producer Rick Rubin uses to help the world's biggest artists transcend and achieve greatness.

Rick Rubin is the co-founder of Def Jam Recordings, the author of the philosophical bestseller The Creative Act: A Way of Being, and has a new documentary called JAY-Z in 8 available on HBO Max and Mubi. Having collaborated with icons like Jay-Z, Johnny Cash, Adele, Tom Petty, and Kanye West, Rubin does not rely on commercial metrics or audience testing to guide his workflow. He shares practical strategies for dismantling the prisons of identity, harnessing the power of "indefensible" ideas, and cultivating a state of pure creative freedom.

*In this episode, he explains:*
■ *The "Indefensible" Act:* Why the greatest artistic breakthroughs defy logic and market research.
■ *The Vacuum Strategy:* How walking away from obligations that no longer serve you creates an energetic void, allowing the universe to present massive unexpected opportunities.
■ *The Audience Trap:* Why focusing on commercial incentives and audience expectations completely undermines the creative process.
■ *Deconstructing the Greats:* The psychological traits that separate good musicians from generational icons.
■ *The Trauma of Success:* Rubin opens up about his crippling depressive episode at age 33 after a corporate restructuring at Def Jam.
