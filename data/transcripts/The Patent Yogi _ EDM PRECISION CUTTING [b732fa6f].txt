How do these two pieces of metal fit together with such incredible precision?

The secret is EDM—Electrical Discharge Machining.

Instead of cutting metal with a blade, it uses controlled electrical sparks to remove tiny amounts of metal.

A thin wire moves through deionized water, while those sparks cut the metal with extreme precision..
