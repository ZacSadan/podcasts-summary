🖱️ This mouse puts your hand in a handshake position—and moves the cursor with your thumb.

Meet this vertical trackball mouse, designed around a 62-degree ergonomic angle with a thumb-controlled trackball, plus wireless multi-device connectivity, adjustable DPI, and shortcut buttons. ⚙️

It looks like a modern redesign of the traditional mouse, but the patent history gets interesting. 🔍

PatentYogi Search Experts found a 2003 Microsoft patent describing a side-mounted, thumb-operated trackball with an ergonomic angle between 40–60 degrees.

That’s remarkably close to the core structure of this product.

So the functional concept may not be new—but the mouse’s distinctive appearance could potentially open the door to design patent protection. 👀
