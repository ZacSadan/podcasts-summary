Sometimes, generative AI can do things so fast that it feels like you don’t have to lift a finger (other than typing your initial prompt). So much so, that many people are tempted to save time by offloading routine writing tasks to their preferred chatbot. But what if you’re better off using it more like a teaching tool and thought partner?

In this video lesson, The Lean Startup author Eric Ries shares his unique perspective on what AI can do to enhance and free up more space for your brainpower, rather than outsourcing it.
