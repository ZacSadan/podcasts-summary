Jana Monroe is a retired FBI Special Agent who was one of the first women to serve in the FBI's Behavioral Science Unit, the organization known for pioneering criminal profiling.

During her 20-plus years in the FBI, Monroe worked on hundreds of violent crime and homicide investigations, helping law enforcement agencies analyze offender behavior and identify serial offenders. 

She later held senior leadership roles within the FBI, including overseeing major field offices and contributing to cybercrime initiatives, before retiring in 2006. 

She worked on several notable FBI serial killer cases such as Jeffery Dahmer and the Rogers family killer.Monroe has written about her experience in the FBI in a book titled "Hearts of Darkness: Serial Killers, the Behavioral Science Unit, and My Life as a Woman in the FBI."
