In 1939, Heinrich Himmler established Ravensbrück, the Third Reich’s only concentration camp built exclusively for women and children. Located just north of Berlin, this massive 9-square-kilometer complex became a living hell for over 130,000 prisoners, including Jewish families, political dissidents, and members of the French resistance. Through extraordinary firsthand testimonies from the last survivors and rare clandestine drawings, this premium documentary uncovers the shocking reality of everyday survival, brutal female SS guards, horrific medical experiments, and the incredible, secret acts of defiance that resisted the Nazi war machine until liberation in 1945.
