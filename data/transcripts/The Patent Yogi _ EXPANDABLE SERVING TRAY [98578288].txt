🍽️🔄 **This serving tray can keep getting bigger as your guest list grows.**

Meet this **modular rotating serving tray**—built around a central U-shaped base with removable food trays that can be added or rearranged as needed.

And the whole system can rotate **360 degrees**, making it easier for everyone at the table to reach different dishes. 👀

But here’s where this specific product gets interesting from a patent perspective. 🔍

PatentYogi Search Experts found a **2013 patent called “Oblong Lazy Susan”** describing removable food carriers arranged on a rotating track, with an expandable platform that can grow by adding sections.

That’s extremely close to the core mechanism of this serving system.

So while the product looks clever and highly practical, its **expandable rotating tray concept may already be disclosed** in prior art.

🎥 **Watch the video** to see why this product scored **8/10 for commercial value**, but only **2/10 for novelty and patentability**.
