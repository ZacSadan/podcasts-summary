Horror writer Stephen King is (perhaps unsurprisingly) a fan of the writing advice “Kill your darlings.” The meaning? Get rid of any parts that don’t move the story forward, even if it hurts to let them go. Project management expert and author Antonio Nieto-Rodriguez says the same principle applies to projects in organizations. By culling excess projects, you help move only the best ideas forward.

In this video lesson, he shares his advice on how to ruthlessly prioritize initiatives and build a collaborative culture so teams can put their best work into each project.
