He's never spoken about this side of his life.

Most people know Druski as one of the funniest people on the internet. His videos have been watched over a billion times, and more than 40 million people follow him.

But it all started in Georgia, making funny skits on his phone.

Those videos eventually went viral and eventually caught Drake’s attention. Since then, Druski has gone from making people laugh online to building his own production company, hosting major awards and working alongside people like Kevin Hart.

But I wanted to know where the funny came from.

Because when you go back far enough, you find a story I don’t think most people would expect.

From a young age, Druski learnt to read the mood of a room incredibly quickly. When things became difficult at home, comedy became a way of changing the energy around him.

As we spoke, I realised he was doing the same thing with me.

He would notice tiny changes in my expression, stop mid-answer and ask what I was thinking. 

After nearly 700 conversations on this podcast, very few people have done that.

It made me understand his comedy differently.

We spoke about:

- The message from Drake that transformed his career.
- What was really happening inside his childhood home.
- Why his brother’s life went in a very different direction.
- Having a gun pointed at his head and deciding to change.
- Netflix and Amazon rejecting him but then came crawling back.
- How comedy saved him from prison.

There’s a moment in the conversation where I tell Druski that our dark sides are often connected to our light sides.

I think his story is an extraordinary example of that.

The awareness that helped him navigate difficult moments as a child became part of the thing that millions of people now find funny.

Behind the person you see online is someone far more thoughtful, ambitious and complicated than I expected.

This conversation changed the way I see Druski.
