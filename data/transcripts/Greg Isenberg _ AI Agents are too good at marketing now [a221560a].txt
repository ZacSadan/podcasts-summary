Key Points

• Agents do marketing's middle work once they have data, platform access, and tools.
• A data pipeline into a warehouse gives agents full-funnel data and keeps them off rate-limited APIs.
• Agents write to Facebook and Google Ads through their APIs and read data only from the warehouse.
• One skill turns a brand URL into five researched, on-brand static ads.
• A UGC avatar ad with motion graphics costs about $5, compared with $300 to $500 from humans.
• Creative velocity wins, since Facebook's algorithm matches good creative to the right buyer.

Numbered Section Summaries

1. Agents are now Marketers

Cody says a static ad used to need a graphic designer and a copywriter, and a UGC video needed a creator, a video editor, and a motion graphics designer. Now GrokBot, Claude Code, or Dots handles that work with code and tokens, and Mirage Tesseract one-shot edits a 90-minute podcast, cutting silence and retakes and tracking the active speaker. I see the job of anyone building a business in 2027 as building the system around these agents, and Cody compares that role to a conductor, or a StarCraft player coordinating swarms.

2. Giving Agents the Data 

Cody warns that agents querying ad APIs directly flood their context, hallucinate, or build their own local database full of duplicates. He uses Airbyte as a data pipeline from Facebook Ads, Google Analytics, HubSpot, and the payment processor into a ClickHouse data warehouse, a Graft partner. With everything in one place, the agent queries as often as it likes and ties an ad all the way to revenue and customer lifetime value.

3. Tools for Agents

The third piece is tools: Kai AI for images and video, FAL for transcription, OpenRouter for LLMs, and Git Leads or Apollo for email and person data. With these, Cody's Google Ads agent checks in real time whether each search term carries buyer intent for its landing page, auditing 5,000 terms in 20 seconds. Graft bundles the data, access, and tools, with a free start and multiplayer access for teams, and the open source route works too.
