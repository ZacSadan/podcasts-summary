[SHOW NOTES]
Yuval Ariav and Michael Eisenberg on how a 1999 Sopranos episode foreshadowed today's debates at Columbia University.

There's a 1999 Sopranos episode — "Columbus Day" — where Tony's daughter comes back from Columbia and refuses to celebrate Columbus, calling it Indigenous Peoples' Day and Columbus a colonizer. Yuval points out that nearly everything being argued on campus today was already on screen 27 years ago. Michael, who teaches at Columbia Business School, pushes back on the popular picture of the school. "You could see things that are being said today that have existed back then, 27 years ago."
