[SHOW NOTES]
Paul Stamets is widely regarded as the father of modern applied mycology for his work on fungi and their applications in human health. We discuss how mushrooms and other fungi affect, and can improve, our immune system; how certain non-psychedelic mushrooms can enhance cognition and brain plasticity; and the use of psilocybin for depression, anxiety, and other mental health conditions, as well as important safety considerations. This episode is for anyone seeking to learn more about the biology of fungi and their medicinal and other uses.
