I built a portable adjustable DC power supply using a Makita 18V battery and the SK90C 90W buck-boost converter. Adjustable voltage from 0.5-36V with voltage and current control.

Parts:
- SK90C: https://s.shopee.vn/3g3sbafpSK
- Jack: https://s.shopee.vn/9fL5kPFRIW
- Power Switch: https://s.shopee.vn/AAHMLRah5q
- 3D Print File: https://makerworld.com/en/models/3342301-sk90c-power-supply-powered-makita-18v-battery#profileId-3797435
- Makita 18V Battery

The SK90C is a 90W DC-DC buck-boost power supply module with a color display, adjustable voltage and current limiting. It can step the input voltage up or down and provide approximately 0.5-36V output, up to 5A within its 90W power limit.
