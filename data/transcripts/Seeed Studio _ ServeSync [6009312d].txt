ServeSync is a smart restaurant ordering and table-management system. Customers scan a unique QR code at their table to access a web-based menu and place orders. Two Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-S3 boards communicate using ESP-NOW to provide food-status and table-cleaning notifications.

The XIAO ESP32-S3 is ideal for this application because of its compact size, built-in wireless connectivity, sufficient GPIO pins, and powerful ESP32-S3 processor. It enables a small, low-cost, and expandable solution for smart restaurant automation. https://www.hackster.io/554105/servesync-smart-restaurant-ordering-and-table-management-cdc3ab
