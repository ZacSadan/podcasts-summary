In the Agents of Change x AgenticAdvertising.org interview series, sponsored by Agnitio, Aubriana Alvarez Lopez sits down at Cannes Lions 2026 with Mike Chevalier, SVP, Advanced Audio and Data Revenue, iHeartMedia to discuss how one of the largest media companies in the world is modernizing sales through agentic AI without losing human oversight.

Mike shares how iHeartMedia is integrating agents across broadcast radio, podcasts, and streaming via tools like Audiograph and MCP to automate planning and transactions. At the same time, he breaks down the essential balance behind their "Guaranteed Human" promise, explaining why human governance remains non-negotiable around three core pillars—fit, money, and law—and how media roles will evolve from manual execution into higher-level strategy.

In this episode, we cover:

• How iHeartMedia modernizes broadcast and digital sales infrastructure with AI agents
• The "Guaranteed Human" promise and avoiding AI fatigue
• The three pillars of human oversight: fit, money, and law
• Why AI won't eliminate media planners—and how roles will shift toward strategy and prompt leadership
