🏍️ **This three-wheeled motorcycle has a trick most bikes don’t.**

Meet this **leaning three-wheel motorcycle**—its front section can tilt into a turn while the two rear wheels stay stable and planted on the ground. ⚙️

The result is a machine that combines the feel of a leaning motorcycle with the stability of three wheels.

But there’s a catch. 👀

We looked specifically at the **pivot mechanism that makes this motorcycle lean** and found that Honda had already patented essentially the same mechanism back in 1982. 🔍

So while the product looks innovative, the key mechanical feature may not be new enough to support a patent.

🎥 Watch the video to see how this specific motorcycle scored—and why an impressive mechanism can still fail the patentability test.
