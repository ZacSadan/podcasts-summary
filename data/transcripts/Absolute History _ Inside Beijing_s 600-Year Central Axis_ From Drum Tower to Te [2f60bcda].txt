[SHOW NOTES]
A futuristic megacity built on a line drawn 600 years ago. Step onto Beijing’s central axis and see the city’s past and present lock into place.

We cycle from the Drum and Bell Towers where time once rang out, over Jingshan’s man-made hill with sweeping views of the Forbidden City, and into halls where dragons, gold, and ingenious dougong engineering still defy earthquakes. Along the way, a 2,000-year-old blueprint (the Kaogongji), a lightning-sparked fire, and drums beaten 108 times hint at a capital designed to mirror the cosmos. Hutong courtyards reveal the artisans who served the palace, while the Temple of Heaven’s open-air altar shows the emperor’s sacred role at winter solstice. Finally, the restored Yongdingmen anchors the axis as the Birds Nest Stadium extends it into the 21st century.

Discover how a 7.8 km spine still orders a city of glass and steel — and why that matters. Watch to unlock the secrets hidden in Beijing’s perfect line.
