🎧🧥 **This hoodie doesn’t just cover your head—it can bring the outside world closer.**

Meet this **audio hoodie** with a microphone built into the outside of the hood. It captures nearby sounds and sends them to speakers built inside, with a volume control and power button right on the garment. 🔊

At first glance, it sounds like **headphones built into a hoodie**.

But the interesting part is how this specific product works. 👀

An earlier 2015 patent already described a hooded garment with built-in speakers and audio wiring—but it relied on **audio coming from an external source**.

This hoodie does something different: **it actively captures surrounding sound through its built-in microphone.** 🎤

That functional difference could be enough to give the invention a stronger patentability case. 🔍
