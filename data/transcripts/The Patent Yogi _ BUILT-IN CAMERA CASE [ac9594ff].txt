📱📸 **What if your phone case could give your Phone a second camera?**

Meet this **iPhone camera case**—a custom 3D-printed case that houses the electronics from a separate digital camera, giving the phone an entirely different way to capture photos.

The clever part? The iPhone’s original camera stays untouched, while the case adds another camera system. 🤯

But when a product looks this unique, the real question is: **has someone already patented the idea?** 🔍

That makes the core concept much less novel—even though the DIY implementation and camera setup are different.
