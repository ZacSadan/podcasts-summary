There are tens of thousands of buildings in New York City, but by 2030, many of them will be illegal to operate as they are.

The NYC's energy grading system, which is part of Local Law 97, requires large building owners to cut carbon emissions on a set timeline toward the city's goal of halving emissions by 2030 and reaching net zero by 2050.

In this episode of NYC Innovates, we take a looks at how that policy is being put into practice, including heat pump retrofits, the Affordable Housing Reinvestment Fund, and the workforce training needed to carry out the work. We feature perspectives from Urban Green Council and green building companies RunWise, Logical Buildings, and BlockPower on what it takes to decarbonize a dense urban building stock.
