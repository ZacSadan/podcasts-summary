A jiffy bag reveal, a healing ledger, and a papal chalice. This is the Grail hunt you weren’t expecting.

From a Shropshire cave trail that led to an eagle statue and a tiny stone ointment cup linked to Mary Magdalene, to Wales’ Nanteos Cup with 19th-century records of “cures,” and a puzzling 18th-century code at Shugborough Hall, we follow the clues that have obsessed knights, explorers, and codebreakers alike. Then the trail reaches Valencia Cathedral, where the Santo Caliz has been dated to the right era and used at Mass by two popes. But does any of it truly prove the cup of the Last Supper survives?

Walk the tunnels of Roman copper mines, compare conflicting photos of a “nibbled” wooden bowl, decode a reversed Poussin in stone, and hear why experts disagree on what the Grail even is. The final stop may surprise you, but the answer isn’t handed over easily.

Watch to decide which evidence holds up and why faith, legend, and archaeology collide on this quest.
